## The starter story

Lumen Lane Tool Library is made up. It is a neighborhood tool-lending group in a place that is also made up, Lumen Lane. Volunteers lend ordinary household tools. In this story they keep a paper sign-out sheet, and they have a one-page public description of borrowing hours and which tools they own.

That public page is part of the story. Do not search for Lumen Lane Tool Library as if it were a real website. Do not invent a street address, a budget, a volunteer roster, or stories about real people.

The starter idea to evaluate:

> Should this fictional library add a helper on its public page that answers common borrowing questions, such as how long a person may keep a drill? What limits should they set?

A fit helper, in this story, would stick to questions the public page already answers, would not collect personal stories, would not invent safety rules, and would show how to reach a volunteer.
