# What a finished brief includes

This file describes the work. It does not assign points, percentages, or a due date. Your instructor handles grading and dates outside this folder, if this brief is used in a class.

A reader who has never seen the conversation should understand your choice from [../outputs/final-brief.md](../outputs/final-brief.md) alone.

## The brief

- The question is specific enough to answer in a short piece of writing.
- The organization is clearly fictional. No real private records appear.
- You consider more than one path. One of those paths is not using the AI approach.
- The recommendation is a sentence you edited and are willing to stand behind.
- Factual claims about the world outside the made-up story point to public pages you opened. Those pages are listed in [../sources/source-log.md](../sources/source-log.md).
- Risks and limits are included. A helper that sounds convenient can still be a poor idea.
- Claims you could not check are labeled unverified or removed.
- The piece is long enough to explain the choice and short enough for a volunteer to read.

## The reflection

[../reflection/activity-log.md](../reflection/activity-log.md) says what you decided, what you asked an agent to do if you used one, and what you checked yourself. If you had no agent, the log says that.

## Sources are public and opened

A source is a page you can open without a login to someone's private system. Official documentation, a public article, or a public help page can count. A link you did not open does not count. A made-up example row in the source log does not count. The links in [../START_HERE.md](../START_HERE.md) explain the editor and GitHub. Use them as sources for the library question only if your question is actually about those products and you read the page for that reason.

## Your decisions

- You wrote the question.
- You accepted or rejected the plan before the draft.
- You edited the final file.
- You gave a clear yes before any publish, upload, or delete.

An agent may help you search and draft inside those boundaries. The agent does not finish the brief for you.
